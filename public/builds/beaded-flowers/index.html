Beaded Flowers

Beadwork by Robyn Adams. 3D models by Quinn Hopkins, Noodin Studio.

Robyn Adams beads these flowers. I modelled them in 3D, bead by bead, and prepared them as game assets for the worlds she builds in Unreal Engine. We started working together at the Banff Centre during the Digital Constellations residency.

Here the flowers grow as a field you can walk through. Each plant in the field is a few flat cards carrying a picture of its beads, the same assets a game engine uses, so the whole field runs in a browser.
